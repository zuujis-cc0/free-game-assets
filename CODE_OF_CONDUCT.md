# Code of conduct

This is a place for people of every background and skill level to share free game assets. Be kind and patient,
especially with people who are new to making games or to GitHub.

- Talk about the work, not the person. Disagree politely and give reasons.
- No harassment, insults, slurs, sexual content aimed at people, or personal attacks, and no posting other people's
  private information.
- Don't offer assets that aren't yours to give, and don't pass off others' work as your own.
- No spam or advertising.

Maintainers may edit or hide comments, close issues and pull requests, and block accounts that break these rules.
To report something privately, open an issue asking for a private contact, without details, and we will get in touch.
